Where to find every Pokémon

Wild Pokémon are new in this port: every family of the first three generations, except the legendaries, lives somewhere in Kanto or the Sevii Islands, and the National Pokédex is open from the start.

This list covers wild encounters only: gifts, in-game trades and legendary Pokémon are not in it. The percentage is the chance of meeting that Pokémon each time you find one by that method.
