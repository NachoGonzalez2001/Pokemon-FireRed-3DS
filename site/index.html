    The project

    The whole game, made for the 3DS

    Built from the pret/pokefirered decompilation, running natively on the console's own processor: no emulator.

    A 3D overworld

    Towns, routes, caves and interiors rebuilt in voxels from the game's own drawing, with lighting, shadows and an adjustable camera. The classic 2D field is one option away.

    Two screens, made for touch

    The world on top; the region map, party, bag, summary, Pokédex, PC boxes and battle menus on the touch screen below.

    Day and night

    Outdoors the light follows the console's clock, or a day that passes in 24 minutes, from dawn to night.

    48 Mega Evolutions

    From Kanto, Johto, Hoenn and Pokémon Legends: Z-A, with the MEGA RING and the MEGA STONES found in the story, and Gym Leaders and Elite Four who use them too.

    A Pokémon that follows you

    The first of your party walks behind you in eight directions, with Mystery Dungeon style sprites.

    Wild Pokémon you can see

    Every non-legendary Pokémon of the first three generations lives somewhere in Kanto, and they can wander the grass, caves and water instead of random encounters.

    Physical/special split

    Each move is physical or special by itself, as in the later games, with its category shown on the move pages. CLASSIC keeps FireRed's rule.

    Options for everyone

    Game speed, exp share, reusable TMs, free movement, renewed or classic trainer teams and more, from the bottom screen.

    Your save stays a GBA save

    The same 128 KB file moves between the 3DS, GBA emulators and a cartridge dump, and every update keeps it as it is.

    How it works

    From your cartridge to your 3DS

    No game content is distributed. The game data is rebuilt from your own dump, on your own device.

    Choose your ROM

    A clean dump of your own Pokémon FireRed cartridge: USA (Rev 1), Spain, France, Germany or Italy. The game comes out in your cartridge's language. It is checked right away, on your device.

    Build in your browser

    The project's own builder runs on this page and creates the game data. Nothing is uploaded.

    Copy it to your SD card

    Download one ZIP, copy its 3ds folder to the root of the SD card and start the game from the Homebrew Launcher.

    3DS installation

    What you need

    • A Nintendo 3DS or 2DS family console with custom firmware (Luma3DS) and the Homebrew Launcher.
    • A clean dump of your own Pokémon FireRed cartridge: USA (Rev 1), Spain, France, Germany or Italy. The game comes out in your cartridge's language.
    • Your console's SD card, connected to your computer.
    • No 3DS? It also runs on the Azahar emulator, sound included, with no extra files.
    Open the web builder

    Prefer to build offline? The Windows ZIP of each release has the same builder as a program, and the README explains how to run it on Linux and macOS.

    On the SD card

    SD:/
└── 3ds/
    ├── dspfirm.cdc          (for sound on a 3DS)
    └── firered3ds/
        ├── FireRed3DS.3dsx
        ├── FireRed3DS.smdh
        ├── firered3ds.pak
        ├── followers.bin
        └── followers-credits.txt
    Your save, firered3ds.sav, lives in the same folder and is never overwritten by an update. Still, keep a copy before updating.
    On a 3DS, sound needs dspfirm.cdc from your own console: dump it once with the DSP1 homebrew. Without it the game plays without sound. On the Azahar emulator it isn't needed.
